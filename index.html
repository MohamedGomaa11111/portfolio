Methodology

The Analytical Workflow

How I approach business questions: a disciplined 8-stage methodology from domain immersion through actionable strategic recommendations.

STAGE 01

Business Understanding

Defining the core commercial challenge, clarifying objectives with stakeholders, and establishing key performance metrics.

STAGE 02

Data Collection

Identifying authoritative transactional data sources, extracting database records, and verifying coverage across historical intervals.

STAGE 03

Data Cleaning & QA

Resolving missing entries, removing duplicate rows, enforcing correct data types, and testing relational key integrity.

STAGE 04

Exploratory Analysis (EDA)

Uncovering statistical distributions, detecting anomalies, calculating correlation matrices, and formulating testable hypotheses.

STAGE 05

Data Modeling

Architecting dimensional Star Schemas, segregating Facts from Dimensions, and optimizing unidirectional filter flow.

STAGE 06

Visualization & BI

Crafting intuitive, interactive dashboards in Power BI with dynamic DAX metrics, KPI scorecards, and focused UX hierarchy.

STAGE 07

Interpretation

Translating mathematical outputs and visual patterns into concrete operational explanations and root causes.

STAGE 08

Recommendations

Delivering defensible, data-backed operational steps that leadership can immediately execute to drive efficiency.

Background & Philosophy

Engineering Discipline Meets Analytical Rigor

I am a Software Engineering graduate from the Arab Academy for Science, Technology & Maritime Transport (AASTMT) in Aswan, Egypt, dedicated to solving complex operational problems through Data Analytics.

My background in software engineering provides a rigorous foundation in algorithms, relational database structures, and clean architecture. I combine this technical background with commercial problem-solving, turning fragmented data into executive decision clarity.

I believe that the value of an analyst is not measured by the complexity of the code, but by the clarity of the insight and the integrity of the data behind it.

Data Integrity First

A visualization is only as reliable as the underlying ETL pipeline. I audit nulls, validate types, and inspect foreign keys before computing a single metric.

Root-Cause Orientation

Moving beyond surface-level aggregates. I use window functions (LAG, DENSE_RANK) and multi-level DAX drill-downs to pinpoint exact operational bottlenecks.

Clarity of Communication

Translating intricate statistical and SQL queries into clear, concise executive narratives that business stakeholders can act upon immediately.
